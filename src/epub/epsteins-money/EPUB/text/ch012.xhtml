About this edition

Epstein's Money brings together seven investigations published by l0g. The EPUB 3 edition was prepared on 9 August 2026 and contains a structured table of contents, an explicit reading order, twenty adapted infographics and text alternatives.

l0g is an independent editorial observatory focused on risk mechanisms, financial opacity and economic systems. Its analyses begin with public documents, distinguish established facts from attributed information and unknowns, and publish both sources and limitations.
