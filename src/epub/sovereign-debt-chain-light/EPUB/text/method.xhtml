Read the contracts, dates and scope

This book reproduces the seven investigations in l0g’s sovereign-debt series, published on 11 and 12 October 2026, with their sources and limitations. Factual examples in the introduction and conclusion refer to the corresponding chapters. The synthesis develops a reading of documented mechanisms and adds no numerical observations.

Publication dates, collection periods and settlement dates describe different stages. Chapters retain their documentary cut-offs, and data retain their reference periods. Contemporary prices do not implicitly update a historical investigation. Comparisons preserve each source’s currency, units, denominators and conventions.

l0g’s illustrative simulations are not observed trades. Institutional stress exercises remain scenarios, and research estimates retain their assumptions. A change following an announcement does not establish its causal effect. Operators’ publications and policy positions remain attributed to their authors.

The forty figures present mechanisms or data with their captions and qualifications. The investigation relies on accessible documents; l0g obtained no executed private contracts, confidential portfolios or original interviews for this series. The glossary reproduces a selection of the French and English definitions present on the website when this edition was produced.

Finding your way through the book

The chapters follow issuance, funding and decisions under stress. Reading them in sequence introduces the terminology as it becomes necessary. The detailed contents then provide a route back to a particular mechanism, while the glossary keeps the essential definitions available offline.

Each figure retains its caption and references. The complete composition helps compare the arrangement as a whole; the vertical composition supports reading on a narrow screen. Hypothetical examples remain identified alongside historical observations. The supporting references belong to the chapter containing the figure.

Reference numbering restarts in each chapter. The notices identify relevant dates and scope. Links between the seven investigations lead to chapters within the book; opening institutional documents and other l0g resources requires an internet connection.
