From a shared secret to access powers

Who can read our messages, on which devices, and with what consequences for other users? This book brings together seven investigations following the copies, keys and decisions that organise access. From the French debate to documentation from Apple, Signal and American and Chinese authorities, it examines investigative needs, the risks of hijacked access and the resources required for oversight. Uncertainty remains visible alongside the evidence throughout.

The opening chapters examine the French proposal, follow a business secret and explore authorisation and oversight. The book then turns to cloud borders, the consequences of an intrusion and alternative routes to evidence. Its final chapter proposes a framework for public evaluation.

Contents, a list of twenty-one infographics and a glossary support return visits to a mechanism. Every chapter retains its bibliography and limitations. Links between chapters and definitions support offline reading; external documents require a connection.
