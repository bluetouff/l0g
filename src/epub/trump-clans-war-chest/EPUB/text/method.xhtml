Reading the records

This edition brings together the six chapters published by l0g on November 26, 2026 in their original reading order. Each chapter retains its references and figures. The source notes identify the relevant passage and the date of consultation, with links to the records online.

An audit tells us what its authors found. A contract sets out an obligation and its terms. A financial disclosure may date a transaction while leaving its timing, decision-maker or realised gain open. Read each document for the right, payment or relationship it can establish. The edition reflects the public investigations, proposals and disputes as they stood at the time.
