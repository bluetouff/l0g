Glossary

US federal budget authority

Authority provided by law for federal agencies to incur obligations and make the resulting payments, subject to the applicable purpose, amount and period of availability.

Chapter 6, reference S01

Apportionment

An allocation by the White House budget office that divides budget authority by time, activity or purpose and sets limits on new obligations.

Chapter 6, reference S56

Obligation and outlay

An obligation is a legally binding commitment to make a payment, such as a signed contract. An outlay records when the money is actually paid.

Chapter 6, reference S01

Rescission

Cancellation of budget authority through legislation. The president can propose a rescission under the Impoundment Control Act.

Chapter 2, reference S13

Custody account

An account that holds money on behalf of its owner. The applicable rules determine ownership and any powers of custody or distribution.

Chapter 3, reference S04

Accelerated share repurchase

An agreement under which a company pays a dealer and receives an initial delivery of its own shares. The terms determine the final share count or settlement.

Chapter 4, reference S05

Stablecoin

A token intended to track a reference value. USD1 targets parity with the US dollar and is backed by assets held for redemptions.

Chapter 5, reference S54

Total return swap

A contract that lets a dealer pass the performance of a security to a client. The dealer can hold the security to hedge its own commitment.
