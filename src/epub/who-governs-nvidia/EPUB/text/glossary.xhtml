Glossary

Export controls

Rules governing specified shipments, uses and forms of access based on the equipment, destination and parties involved.

Chapter 21, S03

End user

Person or company that will use the hardware or computing service. It can be different from the buyer or data center operator.

Chapter 21, S03

Collateral

An asset pledged to secure a debt in the event of default. Initial equipment value, outstanding principal and eventual recovery are distinct.

Chapter 22, S03

Guarantee

An undertaking for the benefit of another party. Its scope depends on the contract and the event that allows the beneficiary to demand payment.

Chapter 26, S14

Equity dilution

A reduction in a shareholder’s ownership percentage when newly issued shares go disproportionately to other investors. Share classes determine the effect on voting power.

Chapter 26, S07

Warrant

A right to buy shares on agreed terms. The exercise price, deadline and any trigger determine the scope of the right.
