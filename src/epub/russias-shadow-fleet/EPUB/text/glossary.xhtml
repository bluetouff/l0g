Glossary

IMO number

A ship’s permanent identifier. It stays with the hull when its name, flag or owner changes.

Chapter 1, reference S02

Registered owner

The person or company recorded as the owner of the ship. Another company may manage the vessel, while corporate records show who holds shares in the owner.

Chapter 1, reference S12

Bond face value

The reference amount of a bond, used among other things to calculate the contracted interest. Payments may be made in a different currency.

Chapter 2, reference S08

Bond coupon

Interest due to the bondholder under the rate, period and payment terms specified for the issue.

Chapter 2, reference S08

Working capital requirement

Funds tied up in day-to-day operations. Inventory and unpaid customer bills absorb cash, while supplier credit frees it up.

Chapter 2, reference S14

Veksel

A promissory note recording an obligation to pay. The terms examined in this book specify settlement in roubles.

Chapter 3, reference S02

Stablecoin

A token designed to track a reference value. The A7A5 issuer targets parity with the rouble and says the token is backed by bank deposits.

Chapter 3, reference S11

OFAC

The US Treasury office that administers and enforces US economic sanctions.

Chapter 3, reference S06

FinCEN

The US Treasury’s financial intelligence bureau. In the A7 case, it issued an alert and proposed a restriction on certain transfers.

Chapter 3, reference S04

P&I (protection and indemnity)

Cover for maritime liabilities to third parties. The policy sets out the risks covered and the exclusions that apply.

Chapter 4, reference S10

CLC certificate

A document issued or certified by the competent state attesting to insurance or other financial security under the Civil Liability Convention for oil pollution damage.

Chapter 4, reference S08

1992 Fund

An international fund that can compensate certain losses from oil pollution when compensation under the CLC is insufficient. Its convention sets out the qualifying conditions and exclusions.
