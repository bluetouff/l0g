Reading the records

This edition brings together four instalments published on l0g on 19 and 20 November 2026. Arrow, the Lidoil loan, the A7 network and the Caroline Bezengi grounding each illuminate a different part of Russia’s seaborne oil trade. They are separate cases. The book follows an economic sequence across them, from ownership to liability.

The chapters retain their figures and references. Each source entry gives the consultation date and points to the relevant material. When reading a document, start with its author and purpose. A classification record, a sanctions authority’s assessment and a receivable in audited accounts each establish different things. The evidence is presented as it stood when the articles were reported.
