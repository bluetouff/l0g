Following the recovery chain

Chapters 1 to 3 follow JLR, its suppliers and insurance from interrupted operations through financing and settlement. Chapter 4 examines the move from those observations to statistics and economic cost.

Chapters 5 and 6 examine services that have to be rebuilt and shared dependencies. Chapters 7 and 8 turn to public risk sharing, then preparation decisions made before an incident. Each investigation also stands alone with its full bibliography.

The glossary brings together the financial, insurance and technical terms used in the book. All thirty-two figures retain their reading notes and sources. Cross-references allow a reader to return to the mechanism behind a number or an announcement.
