Seven concepts for following the dividend

Withholding tax

Tax deducted when income is paid and remitted to the tax authority by the party responsible for collection. For a cross-border dividend, its application and rate depend on the recipient, domestic law and the relevant tax treaty. A treaty may provide a reduced rate or an exemption subject to its conditions.

Beneficial owner

In the tax treatment of dividends, a person entitled to use and enjoy the income without a legal or contractual obligation to pass it on. That status depends on the obligations, actual circumstances and conditions of the relevant tax treaty. Legal ownership of the security and exposure to its performance require separate analysis.

Securities lending

An arrangement in which a lender temporarily transfers shares or bonds to a borrower, who undertakes to return equivalent securities. The borrower generally provides collateral and pays a fee. Lending can support settlement, market making or hedging; collateral and income-related payments depend on the contract.

TRS

A derivative that passes the total economic performance of a reference asset, including income, price movements and credit losses, to another party in return for a financing payment under the agreed terms. Economic exposure can move while legal ownership stays with the original holder. Protection depends on the promised payments being made.

Proposed tax adjustment

A reasoned notice in which the French tax authority sets out intended changes to a taxpayer’s tax position and their factual and legal grounds. Under the adversarial procedure, the taxpayer can accept the changes or make representations within the prescribed period. Proposed adjustments may then be revised or dropped; their amount is distinct from the tax eventually assessed for collection and the cash received.

CJIP

A French criminal-procedure settlement agreed by a prosecutor and a legal person in the cases allowed by law, then submitted for judicial approval. It may require a public-interest fine and other obligations. Approval is distinct from a declaration of guilt or a conviction; fulfilling the agreed obligations extinguishes the prosecution for the covered conduct.

Counterfactual

An estimated outcome that would have occurred without an intervention, allowing for other relevant conditions. It provides a comparison with the observed result and depends on the model and its assumptions. A price decline following an announcement alone does not establish the announcement’s effect.
