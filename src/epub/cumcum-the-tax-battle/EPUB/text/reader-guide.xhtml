From the dividend to the public account

Chapter 1 explains the mechanism through a dividend and its contracts. Chapter 2 follows warnings, tax work and revelations. Chapter 3 examines loss estimates and the categories of money assessed or paid. Together they provide the foundations for understanding the argument about cum-cum’s fiscal cost.

The final three chapters examine the response: judicial proceedings with their different outcomes, the dispute over tax guidance, and evaluation of French and European reforms. Each chapter remains self-contained with its own sources. Reading the sequence connects the financial arrangements to the obligations imposed and the results expected.

Seven glossary entries explain recurring concepts. The twenty-six figures retain their units, periods, sources and limitations. Numerical examples and the offline adaptations of the tools are explicitly illustrative. Synthesis references give the chapter and source supporting the introduction or conclusion.
