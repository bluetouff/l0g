The language of claims and guarantees

Special-purpose vehicle / SPV

An entity established for a particular transaction that can own assets, borrow and enter leases. Contracts determine creditor recourse. Control and accounting rules determine consolidation.

  • Chapter 2, reference S03

Delayed draw term loan / DDTL

A term loan that can be drawn in stages during an agreed period, subject to conditions. The facility limit, amounts drawn and principal still outstanding are different measures.

  • Chapter 2, reference S01

Exposure at default / EAD

A prudential measure of credit exposure at default. Treatment of undrawn commitments and protection depends on the framework and method. It is separate from funded loans and expected losses.

  • Chapter 2, reference S07

Collateral

Assets pledged or otherwise provided to secure an obligation. Eligibility, recognised value and enforcement rights depend on the documents and applicable law.

  • Chapter 3, reference S04

Borrowing base

A pool of eligible assets used to calculate permitted borrowing. Values, advance rates and concentration limits follow the agreement. A decline can reduce available credit.

  • Chapter 3, reference S04

Covenant

An obligation, restriction or threshold in a financing agreement. Contractual definitions, testing dates, exceptions and remedies determine how the clause applies.

  • Chapter 5, reference S04

Letter of credit / LC

A bank undertaking to pay subject to defined conditions and documents. A standby letter generally supports an obligation upon failure to perform or pay. Issuance can consume facility capacity before any cash payment.

  • Chapter 2, reference S18

Private credit

Privately negotiated debt funded mainly by non-bank investment vehicles. Liquidity, leverage and redemption arrangements depend on the vehicle.

  • Chapter 3, reference S07

Loan non-payment insurance

A policy covering defined lender losses from non-payment of an insured debt. The policy specifies insured events, conditions, limits and payment timing.

  • Chapter 4, reference S04

Reinsurance

An agreement under which an insurer transfers risks to a reinsurer. The insurer retains its obligations to the policyholder. Recoverable losses and conditions follow the reinsurance contract.

  • Chapter 4, reference S07

Business development company / BDC

A US investment company category subject to particular requirements, including eligible assets and leverage. A BDC may be listed or unlisted. Its accounts disclose both investments and its own borrowing.

  • Chapter 3, reference S03

Consolidation perimeter

The entities included in group accounts under the applicable control rules. Repeated holdings in a group’s tables must be reconciled before calculation. Two separate funds can each own an identical allocation.

  • Chapter 6, reference S04

Counterparty risk

The risk that a party cannot meet its obligations. For protection or an expected reimbursement, assessment requires payment terms, available security and the promisor’s ability to perform.

  • Chapter 4, reference S09

Significant risk transfer / SRT

A transfer of part of a portfolio’s credit risk under a prudential framework. In a synthetic structure, loans can remain on the bank’s balance sheet. Protection and its recognition depend on the structure and applicable requirements.

  • Chapter 4, reference S13

Cash trap

A mechanism that directs receipts into a reserve account after a contractual trigger. The waterfall retains its payment priorities. Release of funds and draw restrictions follow the agreement.

  • Chapter 5, reference S04

Cash sweep

The application of defined cash amounts to early debt repayment. The base, percentage, trigger and deadline are contractual. A single facility can contain several distinct sweep mechanisms.
