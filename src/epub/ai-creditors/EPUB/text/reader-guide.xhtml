Reading paths and reference points

The six instalments were published on November 17 and 18, 2026. Their order is preserved: Jupiter, European banks, private credit, insurers, contractual cash controls and the creditor map.

Chapters I and II establish identities and instruments. Chapters III and IV follow fund financing and protection. The last two add the timing of contractual rights, then reconcile identified holders.

Each chapter retains its contemporary vantage point. The first four have a November 17 cutoff, the last two November 18. Chapter VI incorporates the ECB Financial Stability Review published on November 18.

S01 and subsequent markers lead to the chapter’s reference notices, available offline. B01 and subsequent markers lead to the documents supporting the introduction and conclusion. External documents require a connection.

The seventeen figures have their own contents list. An offline glossary explains recurring terms. Main text size can be adjusted, while figure enlargement options depend on the reading app.
