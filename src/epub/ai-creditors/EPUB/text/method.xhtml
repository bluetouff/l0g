Sources, method and limits

This November 18, 2026 edition collects a fixed corpus of six French analyses and their six English counterparts. The chapters are reproduced in full. The introduction, conclusion and reading apparatus were prepared for the book from that corpus.

Event dates, accounting cutoffs, source publication and consultation dates remain distinct. The colophon records the editorial freeze. The book’s public release has a separate timestamp.

Accounts, agreements and institutional texts support claims within their stated scope. Companies describe their own transactions. Press investigations retain their attributions. Chapters identify omitted schedules, redacted clauses and unknown parties.

Currencies, units and dates are preserved. Funded principal, undrawn commitments, fair value, regulatory exposure and guarantee limits are tracked separately. The map documents selected relationships, with possible overlaps between amounts. It computes neither expected losses nor a consolidated total of European exposure.

The six chapters contain 87 reference notices in each language. A document may recur in several chapters. Fifteen synthesis documents and glossary references are additional. Historical chapter notices retain their consultation dates.

Opening and cover illustrations are conceptual. The seventeen figures reproduce the chapters’ sourced mechanisms and data. Online articles may receive corrections and follow-ups after this edition.
