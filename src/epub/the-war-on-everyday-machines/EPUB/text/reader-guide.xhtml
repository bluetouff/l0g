From remote commands to stocked shelves

Remote access can disrupt equipment, destroy the value of inventory and leave staff searching for a way to reopen. Seven chapters follow that journey through shops and industrial sites, explaining the mechanisms, identifying the documents and keeping unresolved questions visible.

The first three chapters examine the refrigeration incidents, the functions that control equipment and the authority given to contractors. Chapter four follows the evidence and the limits of attribution. Chapter five separates economic loss, cash and insurance. The final two chapters bring European regulation into the installed fleet, then examine the resources and capacity required for recovery.

Each investigation can also be read on its own with its bibliography. The contents, internal links, list of 33 infographics and glossary support navigation. External documents require a connection. Fictional examples remain labelled beside the calculations; their amounts and durations make the reasoning reproducible.
