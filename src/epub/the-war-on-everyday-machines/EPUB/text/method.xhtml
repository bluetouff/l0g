Evidence, models and the limits of this edition

This edition, dated 4 November 2026, brings together the seven investigations as published on 3 and 4 November. Their documentary research is closed at 3 November 2026; the individual publication dates are retained. The introduction and conclusion are original syntheses based on those chapters. Publication as a book preserves the access limitations and uncertainties recorded in the articles.

Agency alerts, manufacturers’ manuals, European and French legislation, judicial records and company publications serve different purposes. Statements by victims and suppliers remain attributed. Product capabilities provide documented examples, rather than reconstructions of a victim’s installation. Precedents from other countries and sectors remain separate from the 2026 refrigeration incidents.

Findings of responsibility and intelligence assessments retain the status given by their originating documents. This synthesis attributes the refrigeration incidents to no named actor. The regulatory chapter preserves its documentary position on 3 November, including France’s NIS2 transposition and the non-binding status of preparatory documents. European application dates are kept distinct from conditions at individual installations.

The timestamp, loss and recovery-capacity calculations are explicitly fictional teaching examples. Amounts, durations and priorities are assumptions, separate from victim data or market prices. The final chapter’s threshold includes no estimate of attack frequency. The book establishes neither a Europe-wide loss total nor a causal return on preparedness.

Each chapter retains its bibliography: 99 reference notices per volume, with numbering restarting in each chapter. Some notices cite the same document; the count does not represent independent sources. Synthesis references identify the evidence supporting the new opening and closing sections. The glossary explains sixteen useful terms and provides documentary links.

All 33 infographics retain their data, assumptions, captions and text alternatives. Cross-references among the seven chapters and the glossary are adapted for offline reading. A list of figures supports navigation; third-party documents and additional l0g material require a network connection. Cover and opening artwork are conceptual illustrations, separate from documentary photographs.

This investigation uses public records. It includes no original interviews, private contracts or site audits. No third-party industrial systems were queried or tested. The book explains mechanisms and questions for documentary examination; industrial interventions, personal safety and food-safety decisions remain with qualified, authorised professionals. Source digests describe editorial provenance rather than certification of facts.
