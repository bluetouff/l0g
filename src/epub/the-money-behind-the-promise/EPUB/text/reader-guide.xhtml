From the policy to the payment

The first two chapters begin with AT&T pensions, then follow the rights acquired with insurers. Chapter 3 examines the Jackson–Athene treaty. Chapters 4 and 5 trace investments and income between companies, managers and policyholders.

Chapters 6 and 7 examine valuation and capital rules, then the return of risks in the British stress test. The final chapter returns to policy guarantees and protection in France, the United Kingdom and Florida. Each investigation retains its bibliography and can also be read on its own.

The glossary brings together twenty-one terms already used in the investigations. All thirty-four figures retain their captions, units, dates and limitations. Cross-references lead to the relevant chapters and source notices. The synthesis records identify the paragraphs of the introduction and conclusion they support.
