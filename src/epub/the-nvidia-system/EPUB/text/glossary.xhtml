The language of credit, computing and control

GPU

A processor designed for parallel computation. A model name, a chip count and a server count describe different things.

  • Chapter 7, reference S02

Special-purpose vehicle

An entity set up to hold a project, its assets and contracts. Agreements determine which assets and parties creditors can claim against.

  • Chapter 7, reference S01

Credit enhancement

Protection intended to improve a lender’s position, such as a guarantee, reserve or subordinated contribution. Its cost and beneficiary matter alongside its amount.

  • Chapter 10, reference S01

Residual value guarantee

Contractual protection based on an agreed minimum value for an asset or lease. The guarantor covers a defined shortfall, subject to the agreed conditions and recoveries. The contract specifies the beneficiary, cap and trigger.

  • Chapter 7, reference S09

Collateral

An asset pledged to secure an obligation. Its stated value, the loan principal and the amount recoverable after default require separate examination.

  • Chapter 8, reference S03

Debt-service coverage ratio

Cash available for debt service divided by interest and principal payments due over the same period. Agreements determine the precise calculation. Lower receipts or delayed commissioning can weaken coverage.

  • Chapter 10, reference S02

Remaining performance obligations

Contracted revenue that remains to be recognised as the company fulfils its performance obligations. Accounting rules and the company’s disclosed exclusions determine its scope. Customers may pay before or after the services are provided.

  • Chapter 10, reference S02

Export controls

Rules governing specified transactions according to product, destination, user and end use. A licence must be assessed within its precise scope.

  • Chapter 9, reference S06

End user

The person or entity intended to use the equipment. The commercial intermediary, machine owner and buyer of compute services may be different parties.

  • Chapter 9, reference S06

Directors’ and officers’ insurance

D&O insurance covers specified consequences of claims connected with management duties. Policies determine the insured parties, exclusions and limits. A reported limit is separate from an insurance payment.
