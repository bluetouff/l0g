Sources, method and limits

This edition was assembled on November 15, 2026 from the twelve instalments published between November 9 and November 15. The primary-source status check cited in the conclusion took place on November 15 at 12:31 UTC, or 13:31 in Paris. The colophon records this edition’s editorial freeze timestamp. Events, source publication and the book’s public release have separate timestamps.

Regulatory filings, official texts and accessible contracts anchor the reporting. Company and government statements are attributed to their issuers. Press investigations retain their attributions, including reliance on anonymous witnesses or documents examined by journalists without public release.

A congressional request, a complaint and an investigative finding have different standing. The book identifies procedural status and available responses. A chapter’s sources do not amount to an exhaustive search of court and administrative records. Some institutional pages and reporting were only partly accessible during verification.

Amounts retain their currency, period and scope. Debt issuance is a flow. A guarantee cap describes contractual protection. Future rent, orders, collateral values and project budgets represent other kinds of commitment. Adding them requires information about overlaps and maturities that is often unavailable.

The corpus contains 293 chapter reference notices in each language. A document can appear in more than one chapter. Synthesis documents and glossary references are additional. Their consultation dates concern this edition, while historical chapter notices retain their own dates.

The edition ends with instalment XII. Online articles may receive corrections and follow-ups. Cover illustrations are conceptual. Chapter figures explain mechanisms and data attributed to their sources.
