About this edition

This English edition, dated 25 September 2026, brings together the six parts of When AI Asks You to Pay published on 24 and 25 September 2026, their nine diagrams, an introduction and a conclusion. References, dates, attributions and explicitly hypothetical examples remain attached to the text. Some wording has been tightened for the book.

The text reflows to fit the screen. The contents, source references and links between the six chapters work offline. External documents and the online glossary require an internet connection. Image enlargement depends on the reading app.

The cover, panorama and six chapter illustrations were created with AI assistance. They are conceptual compositions, without representing a real personal record or the architecture of a named provider.

No scripts, remote fonts or tracking tools are embedded.
