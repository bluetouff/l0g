**Scott Bessent wants an outsider to run Nvidia. Washington also wants to sell more American technology abroad.** Those ambitions meet as Nvidia’s board searches for Jensen Huang’s successor. The Treasury secretary is calling for a shake-up, Huang has reportedly discussed a comeback, and the administration is said to be preparing a list of national AI champions that includes Nvidia. Public pressure is now bearing directly on the choice of Nvidia’s next leader. [S03](#source-S03) [S04](#source-S04) [S05](#source-S05)

Federal documents explain how Washington can help American technology packages win business: commercial diplomacy, licensing processes and access to financing agencies. Each channel involves its own decisions. Those choices provide a concrete way to examine the prospect of greater government influence.

This thirteenth installment develops [our analysis of state influence](/en/analysis/nvidia-trump-tim-cook-lutnick-state-leverage/) and [our report on the board inquiry](/en/analysis/nvidia-huang-teter-board-inquiry-port-klang/). The first twelve appear in [The Nvidia System](/en/publications/the-nvidia-system/). The new question concerns the intersection of corporate succession and an industrial policy whose instruments were established before the crisis.

## Bessent presses for an outside appointment

In his Fox interview on **November 15**, Bessent called for an outside CEO and a substantial reorganization. He also said he expected answers from the board and outside lawyers within the next ten days. The oral demand raises pressure on directors while leaving the exact response timetable and format open. [S03](#source-S03)

The board has announced a broader search. Nvidia’s **November 12** SEC filing records the board’s **November 11** decisions: Huang’s immediate resignation, Colette Kress’s appointment as interim CEO while remaining CFO, and Harvey Jones’s appointment as chair. The company announced an internal and external search. On **November 13**, the board told CNBC it was selecting an executive search firm and had not formally engaged any candidate. [S01](#source-S01) [S02](#source-S02) [S11](#source-S11)

The difference matters politically. Directors officially retain several options. The Treasury secretary has expressed a preference that could make an internal appointment harder to defend. An internal appointment could therefore become harder to defend to public officials even if it persuaded directors. The effect of that pressure on the board’s choice remains to be seen.

Bessent also compared the situation with a bank and invoked the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency, or OCC, within that hypothetical setting. The analogy conveys the scale of change he wants. His remarks about potentially illicit sales remain questions and suspicions he raised, separate from the findings still awaited from investigations. [S03](#source-S03)

## Huang’s reported comeback talks run alongside the search

The *Financial Times* reported on **November 15** that Huang had discussed returning to Nvidia’s leadership with confidants. One ally described assembling support and seeking White House backing. The full text, read in the FT’s licensed *Irish Times* edition, relies on unnamed people. The dates of those exchanges are unknown. Nvidia and a spokesperson for Huang declined to comment in that edition. [S04](#source-S04)

The account shows the importance Huang’s allies attach to political support. They see the White House as a possible ally in a contest that would first require the board’s agreement. Huang’s return, directors’ approval and presidential backing remain separate, unresolved steps.

For directors, the task extends beyond choosing a personality. They must establish responsibilities, assess practices inherited from the founder and give the next leader enough authority over Nvidia’s commitments. Our [twelfth installment](/en/analysis/nvidia-after-huang-contracts-guarantees-crisis-costs/) examines those contracts and guarantees. A credible succession must allow financial decisions to be reviewed on their merits, without turning each one into a test of loyalty to Huang.

## The AI champions designation is still a reported plan

At **03:59 UTC on November 16**, or **04:59 in Paris**, the WSJ published a report that the administration planned to designate more than two dozen American companies as national AI champions. Nvidia was among the names cited, alongside Amazon, Microsoft, OpenAI and Anthropic. Reuters repeated the report while explicitly saying it could not independently verify it. A formal designation naming Nvidia remained to be obtained in our November 16 document review. [S05](#source-S05)

The reported aim is to help these companies expand abroad against Chinese competitors. The official chronology places that ambition within a policy developed well before Nvidia’s leadership crisis.

**Executive Order 14320 of July 23, 2025** established support for integrated American technology packages, from equipment to applications. Companies must identify target markets, explain the project structure and specify the federal assistance requested. Commerce selects proposals in consultation with other departments. On **July 6, 2026**, the International Trade Administration announced **78 consortium applications**, still subject to review. [S06](#source-S06) [S09](#source-S09)

A consortium brings businesses together around a common offer. A foreign buyer gains equipment, software and services designed to work together. Washington can evaluate the partners and uses within that package. The current official site, branded the *American SI Exports Program*, using the term “super intelligence,” provides for standing teams and teams formed for a particular transaction. The precise relationship between the future list reported by the WSJ and this program still needs confirmation. [S07](#source-S07)

## Public support can work through the buyer’s financing

The program provides diplomatic advocacy, priority handling of certain export-related processes and referrals to federal financing agencies. Faster administration can help a transaction progress. The relevant licensing decision and credit assessment retain their own requirements. [S07](#source-S07) [S08](#source-S08)

[EXIM](/en/glossary/exim/), the US export credit agency, offers a practical example. Its ExportAI initiative, announced on **May 21, 2026** and now presented as ExportSI, makes loans, guarantees and insurance available for eligible transactions. The agency retains its reasonable-assurance-of-repayment requirement. [S10](#source-S10)

A [loan guarantee](/en/glossary/garantie-de-pret/) can protect a lender financing a foreign buyer. The buyer uses the funds to purchase an American solution and repays under the loan contract. The manufacturer benefits from a better-funded customer. Public exposure depends on the guarantee’s terms and the performance of the loan.

This channel explains how influence can operate through customers and projects. A privately owned company can become more dependent on public decisions to reach particular markets. The transactions selected, financing amounts and contractual conditions would show the extent of that dependence.

The executive order also cites equity investments among agencies’ existing financing tools. The recipient of any such investment must be identified transaction by transaction. In the documents reviewed, the general capacity to fund projects remains distinct from a government equity investment in Nvidia. [S06](#source-S06)

## Compliance matters to project selection

The official FAQ lists criminal investigations or indictments concerning export-control compliance involving people associated with a consortium member among possible national-interest concerns. Its wording leaves an assessment to officials. The passages reviewed contain no automatic exclusion of Nvidia or public requirement to replace its CEO. [S08](#source-S08)

This is the clearest documented intersection between governance and market access. The quality of controls, the reliability of information reaching directors and the ability to answer authorities can matter when a project is assessed. Appointing a CEO and obtaining commercial support follow different procedures, yet the same questions of trust may run through both.

The hypothesis of Washington seeking greater influence therefore has an identifiable mechanism: selecting supported packages, assessing their partners and facilitating some financing. The evidence also limits that interpretation. The program predates Huang’s departure, agencies keep their legal powers, and the board officially continues its search. Our review identified no agreement linking assistance to the choice of Nvidia’s next leader.

Nvidia’s next announced milestone is its results release on **November 17 at approximately 21:20 UTC**, or **22:20 in Paris**, followed by the earnings call at **22:00 UTC**, or **23:00 in Paris**. Management will have an opportunity to explain its approach and the continuity the board expects for customer commitments. A consortium designation letter, a license or signed financing would then give federal support a measurable form. [S11](#source-S11)

The political question becomes practical: how much autonomy can Nvidia’s next leader exercise when some markets also depend on export authorizations, diplomatic backing or federal financing? The answer will take shape through board decisions and the conditions attached to individual projects.
