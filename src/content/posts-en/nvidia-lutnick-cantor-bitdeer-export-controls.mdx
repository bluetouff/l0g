**Cantor Fitzgerald can earn fees selling shares for Bitdeer, which is developing a data center equipped with Nvidia systems in Malaysia.** Howard Lutnick, Cantor’s former leader and the US commerce secretary, is involved in decisions over export controls. A *New York Times* investigation published on **15 November** connects these financial relationships with the restrictions he reportedly favors for equipment and Chinese use abroad. [S01](#source-S01) [S02](#source-S02) [S03](#source-S03)

The paper says it has no evidence that Cantor’s interests influenced his decisions. Commerce says Lutnick has complied with his ethics commitments and keeps department business separate from his sons’ professional dealings. Cantor declined to comment, and Bitdeer did not respond to the paper. The dates of those individual responses are unknown. [S01](#source-S01)

This fifteenth installment develops [our examination of state influence](/en/analysis/nvidia-trump-tim-cook-lutnick-state-leverage/) and [our analysis of Nvidia’s customer financing](/en/analysis/nvidia-after-huang-customer-financing/). Placement contracts and ethics documents make the interests more tangible. They also help identify the questions public decision-makers must address when those interests overlap.

## A201’s business rests on future customers

In its **10 November** results, Bitdeer describes A201 in **Johor Bahru** as an AI cloud project with **21.7 megawatts of IT capacity**. The company reports customer commitments covering **more than 70%** of that capacity and representing **over $1.7 billion in expected revenue over five years**. It says it has ordered **more than 100 GB300 NVL72 racks**, with readiness for service and full deployment targeted for the **second quarter of 2027**. [S02](#source-S02)

Those revenue expectations show the commercial stakes. Realizing that revenue depends on power availability, equipment deployment and performance under the contracts. Bitdeer’s **29 September** announcement says it acquired the racks from an unaffiliated third-party supplier on customary contractual terms. The document leaves that supplier unnamed. [S02](#source-S02) [S04](#source-S04)

The NYT cites one person familiar with the arrangement who identifies Tencent as a likely customer. The Bitdeer documents reviewed leave the customers anonymous. That distinction matters when assessing which uses might fall within future restrictions. [S01](#source-S01) [S02](#source-S02)

Bitdeer describes funding its expansion primarily through customer prepayments, operating cash flow and financing backed by contracted cash flows. A customer’s ability to keep using the equipment can therefore matter to a financing partner well before all the forecast revenue has been received. [S04](#source-S04)

## Cantor has a share placement mandate

The commercial link appears in a sales agreement signed on **3 January 2025** and in Bitdeer’s **10 August 2026** prospectus. Bitdeer can sell up to **$1 billion of shares** through **twelve firms**, including Cantor Fitzgerald & Co. This [at-the-market issuance program](/en/glossary/programme-atm/), or ATM, provides a route for raising capital through market sales. [S03](#source-S03)

Bitdeer selects an agent and sends placement instructions specifying the quantity, sale period and any minimum price. The agent can decline. If it accepts, the agreement calls for commercially reasonable efforts to execute the sales. The prospectus provides for compensation of up to **3% of gross proceeds** from shares sold through the designated agent. [S03](#source-S03)

<NvidiaCantorPlacement2026 lang="en" />

The mandate creates an opportunity to earn commissions. Actual fees depend on the sales completed and the agent used. The arrangement also allows transactions in which an intermediary purchases shares as principal. The documents reviewed do not break out the trades Cantor actually handled. [S03](#source-S03)

For the **third quarter of 2026**, Bitdeer reports **$58 million of net proceeds** from its ATM program. That measures the net funding received by Bitdeer. Cantor’s share of the sales and its compensation remain unreported in these documents. Applying the maximum commission rate to net proceeds would mix two different calculation bases. [S02](#source-S02) [S03](#source-S03)

The stated uses of the funds extend across data centers, high-performance computing, specialized chips, working capital and general corporate purposes. The prospectus allows broad discretion. Tracing A201’s financing would require matching actual cash uses with the project agreements. [S03](#source-S03)

## The reach of controls shapes the available market

The NYT describes proposals covering selected countries and future shipments, with exemptions for equipment already installed. It also reports a notification requirement for certain Chinese AI training uses. These are reported features of a proposal; the corresponding regulatory text was not obtained for this investigation. [S01](#source-S01)

A server’s location and its user’s location are separate facts. A company can connect over a network to equipment hosted abroad. Shipping hardware and providing access to a computing service therefore involve different transactions and uses to examine.

<NvidiaRemoteCompute2026 lang="en" />

The scope of a restriction can shift business between operators. A rule confined to particular countries, operators or users would distribute the burden differently. Transitional provisions would also matter for equipment already in place and projects still under construction.

A201 is scheduled for 2027. Any exemption would depend on the final text, installation dates and applicable classifications. For Bitdeer and its financing partners, those details could affect the available customer base, the reliability of expected revenue and investment timing. These economic channels explain why Cantor’s placement mandate belongs in the examination of overlapping interests.

## The family transfer and the limits of the 2025 waiver

On **19 May 2025**, Cantor announced a transfer of interests to trusts for the benefit of Lutnick’s adult children, with Brandon as controlling trustee. The release identifies Brandon as chairman of the holding company, Cantor Fitzgerald, L.P., and Kyle as executive vice chairman. Their family roles at the group are distinct from the leadership of Cantor Fitzgerald & Co., the entity named in Bitdeer’s sales agreement. [S05](#source-S05)

A memorandum signed on **8 July 2025** by White House Counsel David Warrington grants Lutnick a limited waiver under **18 U.S.C. § 208(b)(1)**. It covers certain matters of general applicability that could affect Cantor, BGC or Newmark while regulatory approvals and the completion of the sales were pending. It excludes matters in which those companies are a party or represent one. Meetings with their representatives are also excluded, including meetings on general policy matters. [S06](#source-S06)

The financial disclosure Lutnick signed on **5 November 2025** reports sales completed on **6 October**, following agreements entered into on **16 May**. Commerce certified the report on **10 November**, followed by OGE on **8 December 2025**. The waiver was written for the period awaiting approvals, closings and asset transfers. Its scope and time limit must remain part of any account of policy decisions in 2026. [S06](#source-S06) [S07](#source-S07)

Our [investigation of the family transfer and the reported Tether credit](/en/analysis/lutnick-tether-family-trust-cantor-sale/) examines that separate dimension. The documents reviewed here add the written limits of the temporary authorization.

## Ethics review depends on the decision involved

The federal impartiality regulation, **5 C.F.R. § 2635.502**, addresses matters involving specific parties. Its covered relationships include certain organizations in which an official’s child holds a position. The regulation provides for an assessment of the circumstances and for agency determinations or authorizations. [S08](#source-S08)

A broad rule and a decision concerning a named operator call for an examination suited to their subject. Applying that framework to Bitdeer would require identifying the parties, the financial interests involved and the secretary’s role in a particular decision. The placement mandate establishes a business relationship. An ethics assessment also needs the records of the public decision itself.

This gives a precise context to Commerce’s statement that Lutnick has met his ethics obligations. The sale disclosures and the waiver allow part of that statement to be examined. The relevant ethics advice and arrangements for preserving impartiality in the export-control matters remain to be obtained.

## The Tether connection involves separate instruments

The NYT also describes an indirect interest in Bitdeer through Tether. A Twenty One SEC filing from **September 2025** describes a note held by a Cantor affiliate in Tether’s parent company, convertible into a minority equity interest. Both the instrument and the document’s date matter when interpreting that connection. [S01](#source-S01) [S09](#source-S09)

A **13D/A filed on 30 June 2026** separately reports Bitdeer shares held by Tether International. These documents place [convertible debt](/en/glossary/dette-convertible/) and shares at different levels of the relationship. Any conversion, updated rights and the exact connections between the entities would need to be reconstructed before quantifying Cantor’s indirect exposure. [S09](#source-S09) [S10](#source-S10)

Bitdeer provides a concrete case for examining future controls: an industrial project, expected revenue, a placement mandate and disclosed financial relationships. Transparency depends on the reasons for choosing the rules’ scope and on the safeguards for impartial decisions affecting individual operators.
