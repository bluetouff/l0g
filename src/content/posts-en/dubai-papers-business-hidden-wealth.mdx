**Concealed wealth still has to remain usable by its owner. The Dubai Papers trial examines the services and intermediaries accused of organising that discretion. Invoices and records needed to operate the service can also leave evidence for investigators.**

*Documentary investigation checked through 10 November 2026. Individual responsibility in the ongoing trial remains for the court to determine. The numerical model below is entirely hypothetical.*

The trial that opened in Paris on 2 November concerns alleged organisers and intermediaries of a tax-evasion operation around Helin. The Association de la presse judiciaire calendar lists hearings through 3 December. That is an expected end to the hearings. The court’s decision is a separate stage. <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s05" aria-label="Source S05">S05</a></sup> <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s06" aria-label="Source S06">S06</a></sup>

The economic question extends beyond having a foreign account: retaining control, accessing money and coordinating relationships requires continuing work. An international company can conduct legitimate business. In the case being tried, the reality of services, tax reporting and each person’s knowledge of the transactions require examination. <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s03" aria-label="Source S03">S03</a></sup> <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s06" aria-label="Source S06">S06</a></sup> <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s10" aria-label="Source S10">S10</a></sup>

## Hidden wealth and unpaid tax tell different stories

In the French part of the case, prosecutors cite more than **€200 million in concealed assets** and at least **€54 million in tax evaded over 2007–2018**, according to AFP. <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s02" aria-label="Source S02">S02</a></sup>

The figures cover different scopes and remain prosecution estimates. An asset figure describes wealth; unpaid tax concerns taxable bases and assessment periods. Comparing them requires definitions and the underlying cases. There is no immediate conversion into a meaningful average tax rate.

The same problem arises when estimating a service provider’s revenue. Assets under administration may generate recurring fees, transaction charges or several services. Revenue depends on agreements, charging bases and time. Assessing Helin’s profitability would require invoices and receipts, followed by reconciliation with expenses and payments to intermediaries.

Our analysis therefore examines the service’s functions. It explains how administering concealed assets could generate revenue and evidence, without applying a uniform fee to the wealth cited by prosecutors.

## Separating names, control and access to money

Radio France’s 2019 investigation documents the functions illustrated below. <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s03" aria-label="Source S03">S03</a></sup> We obtained neither the original leak nor the bank records needed to verify individual transactions.

The tax questions differ. Existing wealth raises issues of ownership, income and reporting duties. An invoice entered into a business’s books introduces a new accounting entry, potentially affecting reported profit and creating a cash movement to reconstruct.

Three roles help explain the structure: the apparent holder, the person giving instructions and the person benefiting from spending. They may coincide or be separated. The name on a bank record describes a legal relationship; analysis of powers and transactions adds information. [Know-your-customer checks](/en/glossary/kyc/) and identification of controlling persons under relevant frameworks address that problem, under each system’s own rules. <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s11" aria-label="Source S11">S11</a></sup>

## An invoice can move more cash than the tax it avoids

Consider an entirely hypothetical business with **€400,000** of taxable profit before the disputed entry. Assume a fictitious **€100,000 invoice** is both **recorded and paid**, then wrongly deducted. The artificially presented tax base falls to **€300,000**.

At a rate chosen for this illustration of **25%**, tax calculated on the return falls from **€100,000 to €75,000**, a **€25,000** difference. Yet **€100,000** has left the business’s cash account. The payment and the tax difference measure separate things.

A fictitious expense is legally non-deductible. Adding it back here restores the original base. The model therefore depicts an artificially reduced return rather than a valid tax entitlement. The general rule for business deductions requires, among other conditions, a genuine expense supported by evidence and incurred for the business. <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s13" aria-label="Source S13">S13</a></sup>

The model deliberately excludes VAT, intermediary fees, personal taxes, penalties and loss-relief effects. It reproduces no Helin case. The chosen rate makes the arithmetic readable; an actual historical transaction would require the rules applying to its year and each taxpayer.

Each side calls for different evidence. Bank records help establish payment. Contracts and work products clarify whether the claimed service existed. Accounting records and the tax return show its treatment. Identifying the final beneficiary requires further reconciliation beyond the invoice itself.

## Intermediaries bring an existing relationship of trust

Le Monde identifies three lawyers and an accountant among the defendants. <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s07" aria-label="Source S07">S07</a></sup>

A referral intermediary’s economic role begins with an existing relationship. A client may know their adviser while knowing very little about a foreign operator. A recommendation bridges that distance and makes the proposed service feel more familiar. This is an analysis of incentives, to be tested against each person’s actions and communications.

Compensation terms must be examined against the agreements for each relationship. No general fee schedule or consolidated Helin margin was obtained. <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s03" aria-label="Source S03">S03</a></sup>

Assessing an intermediary’s interests would require the promised service, the source of compensation and the disclosures made to the client. A payment can compensate an introduction, continuing administration or help with transactions. Its legal treatment also depends on the recipient’s knowledge of the facts.

The OECD examines professionals who knowingly enable financial crimes, distinguishing that conduct from legitimate professional work. This framework helps explain the supply of services around fraud. Criminal responsibility in the Paris case depends on evidence and the rules applicable to each defendant. <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s10" aria-label="Source S10">S10</a></sup>

## External secrecy creates a need for internal precision

The owner of assets wants to retain their rights and have instructions carried out. An operator serving multiple clients has to distinguish balances, mandates and transactions to function. This economic constraint explains the potential importance of internal bookkeeping.

A pseudonym may mean little in isolation. Reconciled with an instruction, a payment or an identifying document, it may help attribute an action. A legal separation visible from outside can coexist with management links preserved inside the business.

This analysis describes the possible value of corroborating traces. It assumes no master file authenticated by l0g. Document integrity, context and confirmation of execution require separate checks. An instruction describes an intention; evidence of payment must come from another trace.

L’Obs’s public revelations date to September 2018. <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s04" aria-label="Source S04">S04</a></sup> Publication is distinct from the delivery of evidence to investigators. The address for the prosecutor’s January 2023 communiqué returned a maintenance page during our checks; exact dates of document transmission are therefore excluded from the facts retained here. <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s01" aria-label="Source S01">S01</a></sup>

## Borders change access to information

Published descriptions locate the Emirati operation in **Ras al-Khaimah**, a different emirate from Dubai. Accounts vary on when the Swiss activity moved. We preserve that limit, without constructing legal continuity or a single relocation date from those publications alone. Vey & Associés, a firm acting for the defence, is cited for its own position and presentation of the case. <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s06" aria-label="Source S06">S06</a></sup>

A change of jurisdiction can alter which authorities must be approached, record-retention rules and the conditions for requesting documents. Establishing obstruction would require assistance requests and responses. Their absence from our collection leaves that question open.

The [Common Reporting Standard](/en/glossary/crs/), or CRS, organises financial-account information reporting. Under the applicable rules, institutions identify account holders and controlling persons of certain entities. Reportable information passes through the competent authority to relevant partners. The OECD’s 2025 consolidated text brings together the standard and amendments; it is an unofficial consolidation. Application depends on domestic rules and exchange relationships. <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s11" aria-label="Source S11">S11</a></sup>

Information travels along that reporting chain. The movement of money follows its own transactions. Incomplete identification, an incorrect tax residence or misclassification can obstruct reconciliation between people and accounts. The existence of a standard is therefore insufficient to measure the quality of information actually received.

The OECD’s 2022 review dates the UAE’s first automatic exchanges to **2018** and then describes participation as **non-reciprocal**, sending without receiving. This is a dated finding. Establishing an exchange concerning Helin would require the file, its contents and evidence of receipt. <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s12" aria-label="Source S12">S12</a></sup>

Another date must be kept separate from the present: in November 2025, the UAE Ministry of Finance announced **CRS 2.0 implementation from 1 January 2027**, with **first exchanges in 2028**. At this investigation’s cutoff, those remain announced future milestones. They do not describe a system already operating during the years being tried. <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s20" aria-label="Source S20">S20</a></sup>

Tracfin belongs to a different reporting channel. France’s financial-intelligence unit analyses suspicious-transaction reports, can request information and passes intelligence to the competent authorities. In the relevant cases, lawyers’ reports pass through their bar president. These institutional powers establish no particular alert in this case. <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s16" aria-label="Source S16">S16</a></sup> <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s18" aria-label="Source S18">S18</a></sup>

## The court must attribute actions and knowledge individually

An organisation can deliver a coherent service while distributing information very unevenly among its staff. French complicity law includes assistance knowingly provided. Examining actions and knowledge therefore matters more than a job title. <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s14" aria-label="Source S14">S14</a></sup>

Professional confidentiality likewise requires distinguishing activities. In 2008, the Conseil d’État examined the relationship between anti-money-laundering duties and confidentiality in advice or defence. That general decision and the current framework clarify protections and their limits. Historical allegations require the versions of the rules applicable at the time and each person’s circumstances. <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s15" aria-label="Source S15">S15</a></sup> <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s18" aria-label="Source S18">S18</a></sup>

AFP reports Henri de Croÿ broadly acknowledging responsibility. <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s09" aria-label="Source S09">S09</a></sup> This remains a reported statement by one participant. The judgment and other defendants’ responsibility must be assessed separately.

In 2023, Le Monde reported four convicted clients. <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s21" aria-label="Source S21">S21</a></sup>

The newspaper also reported that two former employees were convicted on 9 June 2026 through a guilty-plea procedure. <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s08" aria-label="Source S08">S08</a></sup>

Those proceedings concern different people and matters. France’s guilty-plea procedure, CRPC, combines a prosecutor’s proposal, acceptance with legal counsel and judicial approval. The approval order can be appealed. We obtained neither the original decisions in the cited proceedings nor their complete appeal status. <sup className="source-ref"><a href="#source-s17" aria-label="Source S17">S17</a></sup>

## Scrutiny must examine the service and its revenue

Assets under administration leave the provider’s revenue unresolved. Assessing the profitability of a secrecy service requires contracts, invoices and receipts, then the corresponding remuneration and costs. Assessing the public results of an investigation also requires separating seizures, tax assessments, court decisions and cash collected.

These checks extend beyond the final client. Intermediaries can contribute expertise, trust and means of execution. Examining their concrete functions should help establish each person’s responsibility.

Discretion sold to a client requires precision inside the provider’s business. Records needed to operate the service may preserve links that its outward appearance made hard to see. This is the trial’s economic and evidentiary importance: examining the alleged organisation and establishing, through evidence, who did what.

## Scope and limitations

This analysis combines institutional sources, dated legal rules and explicitly attributed reporting. We attended no hearing and obtained neither the original leak, the full case file nor bank statements. The diagrams depict reported or general mechanisms; the tax model is fictional. Prosecution estimates remain subject to judicial examination.
