To pay some of its suppliers with the proceeds of its own oil, Venezuela has to get Washington’s approval. Its central bank submits a request. The State Department directs the distribution and the US Treasury carries it out. The executive order governing the arrangement says the money remains Venezuela’s property. [S01](#source-S01) [S04](#source-S04)

A report KPMG prepared for the State Department shows how much passed through the system from January through September 2026. More than $12 billion came in. At the end of September, less than $169 million remained in the active account. Washington retained control over payment approvals along the way. [S01](#source-S01) [S02](#source-S02)

Earlier in *The Trump Clan’s War Chest*, we examined [DOGE’s access to the Treasury’s payment systems](/en/analysis/trump-doge-federal-spending-controls/) and [the fate of frozen federal funds](/en/analysis/trump-frozen-funds-federal-transfers/). Here the question is how much power the White House gains by directing the use of money, including money that belongs to someone else.

## Washington’s approval

The January 9, 2026 order covers certain proceeds from Venezuelan natural resources and the sale of diluents to the country. When a license requires it, a buyer pays into a designated [custody account](/en/glossary/compte-de-garde/) rather than paying the sanctioned Venezuelan entity directly. The terms vary by license. The Office of Foreign Assets Control, which administers sanctions, gives an exception for payments under certain earthquake relief operations. [S04](#source-S04) [S05](#source-S05)

<TrumpCustodyFigure lang="en" kind="custody" />

KPMG describes requests from Venezuela’s central bank and review overseen by US authorities. Most of the dollars released went to Venezuela’s foreign exchange market, where banks get dollars in return for bolivars. Some payments were also approved for suppliers. [S01](#source-S01)

## The September balance

The active account closed May with more than $1 billion. By the end of September the balance was down to $168.6 million. Add interest to deposits, subtract disbursements and set aside the $50,000 left in a temporary account in Qatar, and the result matches the published balance to the cent. The calculation uses a public copy of the State Department’s commissioned report. [S01](#source-S01)

<TrumpCustodyFigure lang="en" kind="ledger" />

KPMG’s figures begin when money reaches the account. Finding out how much an oil trader made before the deposit would require sales contracts and commission terms. In September, senators asked the administration about beneficiaries and risks of inflated prices. Those questions are still pertinent when the account itself balances. [S01](#source-S01) [S06](#source-S06)

The White House also announced a separate deal involving North American Blue Energy Partners, an oil company. It described a potential 35% stake for the Pentagon’s Office of Strategic Capital and oil purchase rights for the State Department. ProPublica reports that it reviewed the signed agreement, which provides for rights to buy shares. The company says an initial shipment reached Texas in late October. To count income in the US budget would require evidence that the shares were held, that they produced income and where that money went. [S08](#source-S08) [S39](#source-S39) [S43](#source-S43)

## Donations in Washington

Venezuelan payments are controlled through custody. In Washington, the National Park Service can spend gifts it accepts for the purposes of the national park system. Its federal account is listed on USAspending. Budget documents describe a permanent funding channel where gifts may still carry conditions on how they can be used. The account is separate from visitor fees, covered in the previous story. [S10](#source-S10) [S11](#source-S11) [S12](#source-S12) [S16](#source-S16)

<TrumpCustodyFigure lang="en" kind="parks" />

On November 24, *The New York Times* reported that the donations account took in $347 million in September, compared with $50 million expected by the Interior Department. The account has been used for projects in Washington. The records the newspaper reviewed left the source and exact destination of the extra money open. The White House budget office raised the account’s projected resources on September 30. Its document combines budget authority and anticipated receipts. Identifying who funded a project requires matching deposits and donor agreements to bills. Park Service policy calls for scrutiny of any benefits a donor might receive in return. [S03](#source-S03) [S15](#source-S15) [S42](#source-S42)

The White House describes its new ballroom as a donor-funded project. The October 2025 agreement says that the Trust for the National Mall forwards indirect gifts when federal officials submit a written request. It also sets rules for keeping donors anonymous within the limits of the law. On August 31, 2026, the Supreme Court stayed a preliminary injunction limiting construction, finding the preservation group likely lacked standing to sue. The stay allowed work to proceed while litigation ran its course. A petition in the case was still on the Court’s docket as of November 26. [S13](#source-S13) [S44](#source-S44) [S40](#source-S40) [S41](#source-S41)

## Federal revenue and public assets

Customs duties normally go into federal revenue. The Congressional Budget Office recorded $173 billion in net customs duties in fiscal year 2026. The figure reflects refunds following the Supreme Court’s ruling on tariffs imposed under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA). The authority to impose a duty and the authority to spend the proceeds come from different rules. A 1935 agricultural law provides an example of an express appropriation. Its Section 32 calculates funding for agricultural uses from 30% of gross customs receipts in the preceding calendar year. [S18](#source-S18) [S19](#source-S19) [S20](#source-S20) [S22](#source-S22)

A [public shareholding like the one in Intel](/en/analysis/intel-mp-materials-trump-portfolio/) can rise in value without a dollar coming into the Treasury. The GAO documents the agreement giving the Commerce Department a 9.9% stake. Finding out if the holding could fund spending would mean checking the terms of sale, the account for any proceeds and the authority to spend them. The executive order on a sovereign wealth fund is at a different stage. It calls for a plan. [S34](#source-S34) [S35](#source-S35)

Venezuela’s oil proceeds show how much control over a payment can matter even when the money belongs to another country. When a US agency spends on its own behalf, the authority has to be traced back to the law and the records tying the money to its destination.
