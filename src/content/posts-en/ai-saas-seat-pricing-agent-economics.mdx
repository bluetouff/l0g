On November 24, Workday reported nearly $150 million in new [annual contract value](/en/glossary/acv/) for its AI products in a single quarter, more than triple the figure a year earlier. The same company warned in its [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1327811/000132781126000058/wday-20261031.htm) that when customers renew for fewer workers, it can earn less. Workday sells software for HR and finance. The number of people its contracts cover matters just as the software is taking on more of their work. [S01](#source-S01) [S02](#source-S02)

The tension shows up on the bill. If an agent saves a customer enough time to cut back a subscription, what can the vendor sell in its place, and how much does it cost to supply? Workday’s new annual contract value expresses signed sales on an annual basis. Its financial statements record revenue as it delivers the service. [S01](#source-S01) [S02](#source-S02)

## How people became a line on the bill

Software as a service, or SaaS, gives customers subscription access to a hosted application. Some vendors charge for each authorised user, a unit known as a *seat*. Others charge by workers covered, transactions or modules. When a subscription depends on how many people use the software, the vendor can grow as the customer hires. A smaller workforce can show up on the bill at renewal. Workday describes generally non-cancellable subscriptions, typically lasting at least three years and usually invoiced annually in advance. [S02](#source-S02)

<SaasAgentFigure lang="en" kind="circuit" />

A support team might use the time it saves to clear a backlog or open at weekends. A [study in the Quarterly Journal of Economics](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjae044) followed 5,172 support agents as they gained access to a generative AI assistant. Resolved cases per hour rose by 15% on average. The study measures work done in that setting. Management can use the gains to handle more requests or operate with a smaller team. In a [US Census Bureau survey](https://www2.census.gov/ces/wp/2026/CES-WP-26-25R.pdf), small minorities of businesses using AI reported either increasing or decreasing headcount because of it. [S03](#source-S03) [S06](#source-S06)

## Vendors start charging for the work

[Salesforce’s Agentforce pricing](https://www.salesforce.com/agentforce/pricing/) follows a customer’s delivery enquiry through the bill. In its example, the agent takes one billable action to identify the customer from their email address and another to retrieve the order. At the standard list price, each action costs $0.10 and the example costs $0.20, before other services. Each action counts as [billable use](/en/glossary/usage-facturable/), and a single case can require several. [S07](#source-S07)

Workday sells annual credit volumes and charges more credits for an autonomous task than for a simple lookup. SAP describes a shared pool for premium AI features, with some exceptions billed per user. Microsoft advertises both individual licences and Copilot Studio credits. Contract terms matter as much as the advertised rate. A prepaid credit pool gives the vendor some of the predictability of a subscription. A growing volume of actions gives the customer a reason to watch the cost per case. [S08](#source-S08) [S09](#source-S09) [S10](#source-S10)

<SaasAgentFigure lang="en" kind="tarification" />

Microsoft sees a different buyer as well. On its [October earnings call](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/events/fy-2027/earnings-fy-2027-q1), management explained that licences often come out of IT budgets, whereas charging for work performed can reach business departments. A head of customer support can compare the price of an agent with the cost of handling requests, as well as with the price of another licence. [S11](#source-S11)

## The cost of each task decides the margin

Take a fictional support centre that pays $100 a month for each of 1,000 seats. The vendor bills $100,000 and keeps $80,000 in [gross profit](/en/glossary/profit-brut/), assuming an 80% gross margin. At renewal, the customer drops 200 seats. The vendor loses $20,000 in revenue and $16,000 in gross profit.

Suppose the agents bring in $20,000 in revenue and cost half that much to run. They add $10,000 in gross profit. Revenue is back where it started. Monthly gross profit has fallen from $80,000 to **$74,000**. Recovering the profit lost on the seats at the assumed 50% AI gross margin takes **$32,000 in AI revenue**.

<SaasAgentFigure lang="en" kind="seuils" />

The example assumes a constant seat price and margin. A vendor’s cost per task can change with how often the software calls a model, how much information it retrieves and how much time people spend checking results. An error can mean doing the work over. The cost of an action and the cost of a resolved case can move differently. The second often tells the customer more about the value of the service. [S07](#source-S07)

## The transition is starting to show in the accounts

Workday’s [subscription revenue rose 12.8%](https://investor.workday.com/news-and-events/press-releases/news-details/2026/Workday-Announces-Fiscal-2027-Third-Quarter-Financial-Results/default.aspx) in the quarter to the end of October. Subscription costs rose too, partly because of third-party hosting. Microsoft reported year-on-year growth in commercial Microsoft 365 seats and a lower gross margin for its cloud business, a broader category that includes Azure. At ServiceNow, amortisation of acquired assets weighed on subscription gross margin. Those costs cover multiple activities. Measuring the contribution of agents would require revenue and cost figures for the products themselves. [S01](#source-S01) [S02](#source-S02) [S12](#source-S12) [S14](#source-S14)

The scope of the reported metrics also changes. Salesforce [expanded the scope of Agentforce](https://investor.salesforce.com/news/news-details/2026/Salesforce-Delivers-Record-Second-Quarter-Fiscal-2027-Results/default.aspx) for its [annual recurring revenue](/en/glossary/arr/) calculation to include additional offerings, including Slackbot. SAP reported growth in a cloud business that covers more than its Joule agents. Our [breakdown of Microsoft’s accounts](/en/analysis/microsoft-ai-profit-segments-capex-cash-flow/) explains how to compare results across different reporting categories. [S12](#source-S12) [S15](#source-S15) [S17](#source-S17)

The real test comes when contracts are up for renewal. The customer can judge the price of a resolved case, including checks and rework. The vendor will find out how much profit it keeps from the tasks it bills for once seat counts are renegotiated. That is where better productivity can become a good deal for both sides or shift value from one to the other. [S02](#source-S02) [S07](#source-S07) [S11](#source-S11)
