# Artwork for the second Nvidia volume

The two original conceptual illustrations were generated on 25 November 2026 for *Qui gouverne Nvidia ?* and *Who Governs Nvidia?*.

- `nvidia-volume-two-cover-art.png` is the shared portrait master. A silicon chip and its copper traces lead toward a boardroom ledger, server racks, civic architecture and a port.
- `nvidia-volume-two-panorama.png` is the shared landscape master. The boardroom table connects the chip, open ledger and industry visible through the windows. Its darker left side provides space for the social-card text.

These scenes are editorial illustrations. They do not depict original documents, a specific facility or actual Nvidia hardware.

Run `node scripts/generate-nvidia-volume-two-artwork.mjs` after changes to the portrait or landscape master. It produces the localized EPUB and catalogue covers, the responsive panorama and the untitled art for the publication social renderer.
