Les trois images ont été créées avec imagegen le 4 novembre 2026 pour l’Hebdo l0g. Ce sont des illustrations conceptuelles sans texte, ni données chiffrées, ni représentation documentaire. Elles restent attachées aux éditions historiques et ne modifient aucun fait, source ou date de revue.

## Atlas et l’entrepôt de créances

Fichier : `public/illustrations/hebdo/atlas-warehouse-v1.jpg`

Prompt :

Use case: editorial-concept. Asset type: text-free landscape magazine illustration for l0g.fr's weekly issue about Atlas, Apollo and Athene, warehouse credit waiting for securitization. Create an exceptionally elegant editorial still life: long orderly rows of thin ivory paper folios stored in a beautifully detailed miniature warehouse of forest-green translucent glass, brass shelving, one narrow exit portal with folios patiently accumulating in front. Suggest temporary storage and dependence on the exit, no catastrophe. Tactile paper edges, delicate architecture, soft jade reflections, warm pale brass light, rich charcoal shadows. Premium financial magazine photography of a sculptural physical model, sophisticated, fine detail, visually arresting and restrained. Landscape 1.91:1, a strong subject at the center that also survives a portrait crop, generous uncluttered outer margins. No text, no letters, no numerals, no logos, no charts, no arrows, no watermark, no people, no coins. This is a conceptual illustration, not a documentary photograph or a factual financial chart.
